# Recursos

## Fotografía decorativa provisional

- Archivo: `public/images/birthday-still-life.webp`.
- Origen: herramienta integrada `image_gen.imagegen`.
- Optimización: copia WebP local de 1200 × 1200, calidad 88.
- Uso: imagen decorativa provisional para portada fotográfica, collages y Polaroid; pendiente de fotos reales.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: one square editorial still-life photograph for a blush pink birthday invitation website, to serve as a clearly provisional decorative photograph while the host's personal photos are unavailable. Scene: on a pale dusty-pink seamless studio backdrop, a beautiful small vintage heart-shaped pink birthday cake with ornate piped cream rosettes, silk pink bows, two reflective rose-pink heart balloons behind the cake, and a single clear stemmed glass with pale pink lemonade. Soft flash photography, delicate film grain, warm creamy highlights, elegant feminine romantic birthday scrapbook aesthetic. Composition: cake at lower left, balloons at upper right, layered and naturally balanced, objects have room around them for multiple vertical and square crops. Realistic frosting and satin textures, not CGI. No people, no words, no lettering, no numbers, no watermark. Colors: warm ivory, blush #f7b4c8, dusty rose #d47b83. This is a photo asset only, not a website screenshot or collage sheet.
